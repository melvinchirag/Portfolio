Liquid hero buttons

Same raymarched mercury as the menu, now on the two hero buttons. Three behaviours, all live. Hover each button, leave it, press and hold it, and tab to it. Tell me A, B or C, then I roll the pick across every hero button.

Reduced motion is on: the real site fills instantly with no morph. This board still animates so you can compare.

Pool

The sand starts as a drop at your cursor and swells until it floods the button. On the filled button it bulges toward you. Leaving drains it back to where you left.

Bridge

Two drops, one leading and one lagging, stretch a liquid bridge from where you entered to where you are, the same trick as the menu. They merge into the button as you settle.

C

Press ripple

A calm liquid surface. The highlight slides under your cursor, and a press sends a ring across it while the button squashes. No drops, the quietest option.

Quietest of the three

What "fluid" means here

  • Springs with a little overshoot, never fixed-time fades.
  • Drops chase the cursor with lag, never snap to it.
  • A squash on press and a soft release.
  • No hard cut between any two states.

Where the pick goes

  • Big buttons: See the work, Contact. Full liquid pill.
  • Project rail arrows and the frame dots: a small drop that swells.
  • GitHub, LinkedIn, Resume, Read the full story: a thin liquid underline that flows to the cursor.
  • Nothing runs until someone touches a button. No WebGL means today's hover. Reduced motion means an instant fill.
